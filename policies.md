# NafPT proposal policies
(نص عام مؤقت. لو عندك سياسات نافذلي الرسمية الصق بنودها هنا، وهتتحط تلقائيًا في كل بروبوزال.)
- No phone numbers, WhatsApp/Telegram/social handles or emails inside the proposal.
- No external links.
- Never ask the client to talk, agree or pay outside the platform.
- Never promise guaranteed results; never claim experience, certificates, clients or testimonials that are not in the profile.
- Never claim senior level or specific years of experience.
- Do not copy the project text verbatim and do not write a generic proposal that fits any project.
- Never disparage competitors; no offensive language.
- The proposal language must match the client's language (Arabic or English).
